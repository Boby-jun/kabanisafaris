Who I Am

A Consultant Rooted in Africa

After four years living in East Africa and based in Geneva, Switzerland, I built a deep network of local guides, operators, and farmers who know their terrain like the back of their hand. Kabani Safaris is not a booking platform — it is a consultancy that matches your curiosity with the right people on the ground.

"Every itinerary is built from scratch. The goal is always the same: a memory that outlasts the journey."

Our Philosophy

Three principles that guide every journey

Tailor-Made by Design

No two travellers are the same. Every itinerary is built from zero around your pace, interests, and budget — then refined until it fits perfectly.

Local Professionals

We connect you exclusively with guides and operators who live and breathe their territory. Deep knowledge is not taught in a classroom — it is inherited.

Impact-Led Travel

Where possible, we route itineraries through cooperatives, community farms, and independent operators so that tourism becomes a direct economic engine for local producers.
